About website

Architecture & philosophy

The framework

The design philosophy for this site is neo-brutalism, which is characterized by bold, unapologetic use of color, raw textures, and a focus on functionality over form. The framework used to build this site is vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no build step or dependencies. This approach allows for maximum control over the design and performance of the site while adhering to the brutalist ethos of simplicity and rawness.

Interaction design

A more personal reason for this design methodology and color pallette is my deep and unfiltered love of coffee. The colors of this site are inspired by the rich, earthy tones of coffee, with a palette that includes deep browns, warm ambers, and vibrant emeralds. The design is meant to evoke the feeling of sipping a perfectly brewed cup of iced latte while browsing through my projects and learning about my journey as a developer.

Typography and color

The typography choices for this site are Space Grotesk for headings and Space Mono for body text. Space Grotesk is a modern, geometric sans-serif typeface that adds a bold and contemporary feel to the design, while Space Mono is a monospaced font that provides a clean and readable texture for the body copy. The color palette of deep browns, warm ambers, and vibrant emeralds is used consistently throughout the site to create a cohesive and immersive experience that reflects my personal style and love of coffee.
